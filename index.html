Research practice

Digital evidence. Human stories. Public interest.

OSINTData journalismDisinformationCyber-enabled crimeDigital verification

My approach combines journalistic investigation with open-source research and careful verification. The goal is to explain not only what happened, but what the available evidence supports, what remains uncertain and why it matters.

  1. 01
    Collect and preserveIdentify relevant public sources, record provenance and keep a clear research trail.
  2. 02
    Verify and corroborateCross-check claims, dates, identities and digital material using independent sources where possible.
  3. 03
    Analyse connectionsUse structured data and open-source techniques to test patterns without overstating what they prove.
  4. 04
    Report responsiblyExplain methods and limitations, seek responses and protect sources and sensitive information.
About

Journalism with a digital investigations lens.

Olayinka Shehu is an investigative journalist and researcher working across scams, disinformation, influence operations, open-source intelligence (OSINT) and data journalism. The work focuses on digital threats and public-interest reporting in Nigeria and West Africa.

Reporting has appeared in The Cable, FIJ, Economy Post, The ICIR, The Nation and Nigerian Tribune. Olayinka is interested in collaborations with editors, investigative newsrooms and research organisations examining information integrity and cyber-enabled crime.
